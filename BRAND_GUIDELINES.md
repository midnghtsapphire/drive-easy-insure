# Brand Guidelines — ReinstatePro

## Brand Core
- **Brand name:** ReinstatePro
- **Tagline:** "Getting you back on the road, legally."
- **Brand personality:** trustworthy, non-judgmental, fast, clear

## Positioning
ReinstatePro helps drivers navigate a stressful, compliance-heavy insurance requirement without shame or confusion. The brand should feel like a calm expert: decisive, respectful, and focused on solutions.

## Color System
Map the brand to the Tailwind tokens already used in the app.

- **Primary / Deep Navy** → `primary`
  - Use for headers, footer surfaces, and core trust anchors
- **Accent / Electric Blue** → `accent`
  - Use for CTA buttons, highlights, active selections, and focus moments
- **Success Green** → `success`
  - Use for confirmations, compliance reassurance, and positive status indicators
- **Background / Card / Border** → `background`, `card`, `border`
  - Preserve the dark insurance aesthetic with clear contrast and premium polish

## Typography
- **Headings:** Use the product's bold display style with strong weight and tight hierarchy
- **Body:** Use clean sans-serif body text optimized for readability on mobile and desktop
- **Recommended pairing:** Inter or system sans for body, with a bold geometric sans presentation for headings where available

## Logo Usage
- The logo mark is the ReinstatePro "R" badge used in the app shell
- Maintain generous clear space around the mark
- Use the full wordmark on dark surfaces whenever space allows
- Ensure contrast meets accessibility standards

### Do
- Use the mark on navy or dark neutral backgrounds
- Keep proportions consistent
- Pair the mark with the tagline only in marketing contexts

### Don't
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Add glow effects that reduce legibility
- Place the logo on busy photography without a dark overlay
- Swap brand colors for unrelated palettes

## Voice & Tone
- **Empathetic:** acknowledge urgency without sounding alarmist
- **Direct:** explain next steps clearly and quickly
- **Authoritative:** speak with compliance confidence and operational clarity

## Content Guidelines
- Avoid shame language around DUI, suspension, or prior violations
- Focus on solutions, next steps, timelines, and compliance outcomes
- Prefer phrases like "license reinstatement", "same-day filing", and "get covered fast"
- Keep policy, filing, and billing language transparent and plain-English

## Visual Style
- Dark, professional insurance aesthetic
- Premium cards, clear data hierarchy, and reassuring checklists
- Minimal clutter, strong CTA contrast, and mobile-first layout discipline
