# Go-To-Market Strategy — ReinstatePro

## Executive Summary

ReinstatePro targets the high-intent SR-22 insurance segment: drivers who must quickly secure proof of financial responsibility to reinstate driving privileges. The market is large, urgent, and underserved by modern digital experiences. With an estimated 4.2 million active SR-22 filers in the United States, an average filing duration of three years, and average premiums around $1,200 annually, the total addressable market exceeds $5 billion.

ReinstatePro's positioning is built on speed, empathy, and compliance. Rather than treating drivers as generic high-risk leads, the platform frames the experience around immediate reinstatement outcomes, same-day filing, and guided checkout. The initial launch focuses on California, Colorado, and North Carolina, then expands into adjacent high-demand states and FR-44 markets.

## TAM / SAM / SOM Analysis

### TAM — Total Addressable Market
- 4.2M active SR-22 filers nationwide
- Average annual premium: ~$1,200
- Average filing period: ~3 years
- Estimated TAM: $5B+ annually in premium volume

### SAM — Serviceable Available Market
Initial state coverage narrows the near-term market to California, Colorado, and North Carolina.
- California: 800K+ active or recently active SR-22-related drivers
- Colorado: meaningful reinstatement and non-owner demand driven by DUI/uninsured incidents
- North Carolina: consistent high-risk filing demand with strong broker opportunity
- Combined SAM estimate: ~$900M–$1.2B annual premium opportunity

### SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market
With a lean digital funnel, paid acquisition, and referral channels, ReinstatePro can target:
- Year 1: 300–1,000 active policyholders
- Year 2: 2,000–4,000 active policyholders
- Year 3: 6,000–8,000 active policyholders plus lead resale and carrier revenue
- Revenue path toward $10M ARR combines subscription policies, lead generation, and commission income

## Target Customer Personas

### 1. Urgent Reinstatement Driver — "Marcus"
- Age: 32
- Income: $42K/year
- Trigger: DUI conviction and license suspension
- Need: SR-22 filed fast to drive to work again
- Behavior: Searches on mobile, after-hours, high urgency, compares 2–3 providers max
- Messaging: "Get back on the road today. Same-day SR-22 filing."

### 2. Non-Owner Recovery Driver — "Danielle"
- Age: 27
- Income: $36K/year
- Trigger: Driving uninsured, no personal vehicle owned
- Need: Non-owner SR-22 to restore license affordably
- Behavior: Price sensitive, often asks family or legal contacts for recommendations
- Messaging: "Affordable non-owner SR-22 coverage without owning a car."

### 3. Family Stabilizer — "Carlos"
- Age: 45
- Income: $68K household
- Trigger: At-fault accident or repeated violations
- Need: Reliable policy, predictable monthly payment, fast proof for court/DMV
- Behavior: Desktop research, values trust signals, wants human support if checkout stalls
- Messaging: "Clear pricing, compliant coverage, and guided help from start to filing."

## Revenue Model Breakdown

1. **Policy subscriptions**
   - Monthly plans from $79 to $159
   - Recurring revenue from SR-22 policyholders
2. **Lead generation fees**
   - Sell qualified quote leads to licensed carriers and agencies
   - Estimated $8–$25 per qualified lead
3. **Carrier commissions**
   - 12–18% of first-year premium depending on arrangement
4. **Future upsells**
   - FR-44 filings
   - Higher-limit policy upgrades
   - Policy add-ons like roadside, rental, gap

## Launch Strategy

### SEO
- Publish state-specific landing pages for CA, CO, and NC
- Create educational content around DUI reinstatement, non-owner SR-22, filing timelines, and cost comparisons
- Build local pages around DMV office areas and court-heavy metros

### PPC
- Capture bottom-funnel search terms like "sr22 insurance", "cheap sr22 insurance", and "non owner sr22"
- Use state and device segmentation to prioritize mobile intent
- Create dedicated landing pages per keyword cluster

### Social Media
- Short-form educational explainers about license reinstatement steps
- Retarget site visitors with urgency-based creative
- Build trust with customer stories, FAQs, and filing myths

### Referral Partnerships
- DUI defense attorneys
- Court-mandated traffic schools
- Bail bond agencies
- Independent agencies that do not want to service subscale SR-22 demand
